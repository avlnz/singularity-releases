SINGULARITY

A sci-fi action RPG. One shared city, Port Halden, and the machine-built depths under it. You need an invite to play: sign in with the email address you were invited with.

Download for MacLauncher, about 60 MB Download for WindowsLauncher, about 40 MB

Wait for the download to finish before opening it. The launcher then downloads the game itself (about 500 MB the first time) and keeps it up to date.

Getting in

  1. Unzip and open the launcher. On Mac, drag it into Applications first. On Windows, the first run may warn about an unknown publisher: choose More info, then Run anyway.
  2. Press Sign in with email. Your browser opens. Type the email address you were invited with.
  3. Click the link in the email you get. Then go back to the launcher. If it is still waiting, press Open the browser again.
  4. Press Play. You land in Port Halden with everyone else who is online.
