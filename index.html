Getting in

  1. Open the launcher. Let the download finish, then unzip it. On a Mac, move it into Applications. Windows may warn about an unknown publisher: choose More info, then Run anyway.
  2. Type your invite code. There is no account to create. The code works once, on one computer. Invited by email instead? Choose "Sign in with email instead".
  3. Wait for the download, then press Play. The first download is about 500 MB. The launcher installs the game and keeps it up to date. You arrive in Port Halden with everyone who is online.
