Singularity

A sci-fi action RPG. Everyone shares one city, Port Halden, and fights through the machine-built depths under it, alone or in a party.

Download for Mac Download for Windows

A small launcher (about 60 MB on Mac, 40 MB on Windows). It installs the game, about 500 MB, and keeps it up to date.

Getting in

  1. Open the launcher. Let the download finish, then unzip it. On a Mac, move it into Applications. Windows may warn about an unknown publisher: choose More info, then Run anyway.
  2. Sign in with email. Use the address you were invited with.
  3. Click the link in the email, then go back to the launcher.
  4. Press Play. You arrive in Port Halden with everyone who is online.
