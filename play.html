Settings has a second mouse mode, Classic, where left click attacks as well as moves.

On a controller

The whole game plays on a controller: plug one in and the on-screen prompts switch to its buttons, shown with Xbox-style names. Three things still want a keyboard or the pause menu:

  • Skill tree and players list are in the pause menu, under Skills and Players.
  • Party invites: walk up to whoever invited you and press X to join.
  • Chat needs a keyboard.

Playing together

Everyone who is online is in the same Port Halden. You see each other in the streets and on the minimap, and chat reaches everyone.

  • Party up in one press. Walk up to a player and press F. They get a banner and press Y to join you, or N to pass.
  • Or pick from the list. O shows who is online and where, with Invite and Join beside each name.
  • In a party you follow the leader down the lift and fight the same machines. Your loot is your own: machines you helped kill drop for you.
  • Leaving is in the players list.

What you do

  • Port Halden is the city: traders, other players, your ship, and the freight lift down. The minimap always points to the lift.
  • Contracts start at the lift's board: three floors down with a boss at the bottom. There are five tiers, and clearing one opens the next.
  • Raids open for a tier once you have cleared its contract: five floors, two bosses and more loot.
  • The endless depths are on the same board, for going as deep as you can.
  • Levels and skills. Kills and finished runs give XP. Every level gives a skill point to spend in the skill tree (K): Radiant, Prism or Void.
  • The Nightjar is your ship on the landing pad, with the armory, the workbench and the crystal vault.

Early access

This is an early build, so things will break. Tell whoever invited you what you ran into: what you were doing, and what you saw.
