14 November 2026Versions 2026.1114.1629, 2026.1114.1624, 2026.1114.1609

Updates from inside the game, steadier co-op, less loot

New
  • A running game tells you when a newer version is out. RESTART AND UPDATE in the pause menu closes it, updates and starts it again.
Better
  • Co-op: vault doors, relays, lockers, cases and coolant bays are in the same places for everyone in a party.
  • Players list: nobody can join your world without your invite.
  • Very crowded deep floors have more room for effects, so shots and blasts are less likely to go missing or draw wrongly.
Balance
  • Loot: a little less of it and far fewer Rare (yellow) drops, with more Uncommon and Epic.
  • Ordinary machines drop gear about one kill in 55 (was 40). A champion's second piece comes one time in five (was three), and champions no longer always drop Rare or better.
Fixed
  • The jam signal is started by the party leader, so a party member can no longer skip its waves and still collect the payout.

14 November 2026Versions 2026.1114.1558, 2026.1114.1547

See your party fight, a steadier picture on Mac

New
  • Co-op: you see other players shoot, with muzzle flashes and tracers along their aim.
  • The players list and the status line say whether you are in a party and who leads it.
  • Upscaling setting: Auto, Stable or Sharp.
  • Slipstream, a Prism skill that shortens the dodge cooldown.
Better
  • Taking the lift to a depth a recent party member is already on puts you on their floor.
  • Armory: a weapon you take goes to your bag, and what you hold stays in your hands.
Balance
  • Dodge has a 1.6 second cooldown (was 0.55), with a small ring when it is ready again.
Fixed
  • On a Mac the picture uses a steadier upscaler by default.
  • The Nightjar's wall panels and the city's street fronts and columns are solid, so nobody stands inside them.

14 November 2026Versions 2026.1114.1538, 2026.1114.1523, 2026.1114.1502

Invite codes and an easier first launch

New
  • Invite codes: type a code in the launcher once and you are in. No account or email needed.
  • The launcher has a How to play button.
  • The game can report errors to help fix them.
Better
  • First launch: the launcher starts the download by itself, says what it is getting and that Play comes next.
  • Inventory: an item's details sit beside the panel, always on screen, next to what you have equipped in that slot.
  • Inventory cards are nearly opaque and easier to read.
  • With another player in your world, opening a menu no longer pauses the game for everyone. Alone, it pauses as before.
Fixed
  • Player names show the name you were invited as, or your computer's user name, instead of everyone on Windows being Pilot.

14 November 2026Versions 2026.1114.1131, 2026.1114.1053

A new opening

New
  • The game starts on a fight: First Contract is the first quest, and its marker points at the freight lift.
Better
  • Shore Leave, the tour of the ship and the city, opens after your first run as side work.
  • First Contract's last step, learning a skill, can be done anywhere.
  • Coming home from a run puts you back at the entrance you left by: freight lift, raid gate or deep shaft.

14 November 2026Versions 2026.1114.0148, 2026.1114.0126

Arc Blade and legendary powers that change builds

New
  • Arc Blade, an energy sword. It drops as loot, sits in a weapon slot and is on the armory's close range rack.
  • Arc Blade stances on Z or the right stick: Light (a fast three-swing chain), Balanced (wide arcs that catch a group) and Heavy (two slow swings that stagger, then a slam).
  • The last swing of a chain is a finisher powered by your alignment: Radiant throws a wave of light and raises a ward, Void drains what it hits, and balance returns Current.
  • Eleven new legendary powers, 17 in all.
  • Overdrive, Glass Cannon, Momentum, Executioner, Shrapnel and Ward Engine change how you fight.
  • Sunforged, Afterimage, Long Reach and Resonance rebuild a skill around the weapon: a free Sunlance, Phase Step reset on a kill, Void Lash on three machines, a faster Kinetic Pulse.
  • Sweeping Edge, a legendary power for blades only.
Better
  • Legendaries you already own keep the power they had.

14 November 2026Versions 2026.1114.0044, 2026.1114.0022

Floor conditions and best times

New
  • Floor conditions: about two run floors in three roll a twist: Overclocked, Plated, Unstable cores, Rich seam, Champion nest, Current surge or Brittle.
  • The condition shows beside the depth and is announced when you arrive. Harder ones pay more XP or better finds.
  • Results show your best time for each tier's contract and raid.
Better
  • Launcher: a new eclipse backdrop whose glow shows what it is doing: emerald when the game is ready, amber while checking or downloading, red when something failed.

13 November 2026Versions 2026.1113.2300, 2026.1113.2221

Three ways down, a new Nightjar interior

New
  • Three ways down, in three parts of the city: contracts at the freight lift, raids from a raid gate and the endless depths from a deep shaft.
  • Each way down has its own lift platform and light colour, and the minimap points to each by name: CONTRACTS, RAIDS, DEPTHS.
Better
  • The Nightjar's interior is rebuilt in white panels with gold trim, with windows on the hull walls, new shelves, consoles and capsules.
Fixed
  • Grey squares no longer show through the Nightjar's floor.

13 November 2026Versions 2026.1113.1850, 2026.1113.0211

Playing together

New
  • Players list on O and in the pause menu: who is online and where, with Invite beside each name.
  • Notices when a player comes online or goes down on a run.
  • The minimap always points to the way down and to every other player in the zone.
Better
  • One press on another player invites them. An invite is a banner you answer with Y or N.
Fixed
  • Wall lamps on run floors no longer glow as a bright slab through walls, and gunfire no longer makes the fog strobe.

12 November 2026Version 2026.1112.1640

Skill tree and raids

New
  • Skill tree on K and in the pause menu: three branches, three tiers and 18 skills.
  • Every level gives a skill point. Passives take three ranks, and each tier opens with points spent in its branch.
  • New passives: Vigour, Zeal, Reserve, Fleet, Focus, Ruin and Dread.
  • Raids: clear a tier's contract to open its raid. Five floors, a Relay Warden holding the lift on floor 3 and the Overseer on floor 5.
  • Raids pay more XP, better loot luck and 4 or 5 vault pieces.

12 November 2026Version 2026.1112.1350

Speak to chat

New
  • Hold V to speak a chat line. It is turned into text on your own computer and lands in the chat line for you to check; Enter sends it.
  • The speech model (57 MB) downloads once, the first time you use it.

11 November 2026Versions 2026.1111.2147, 2026.1111.1949

Chat, parties and a new quest log

New
  • Chat: Enter opens a line, and everyone online sees it with your name and where you are. You stand still while typing.
  • Parties: walk up to another player in a safe zone to invite them. They answer yes or no and join you.
Better
  • Quest log: cards in two columns with a progress bar, ticked steps, the current step in gold with where to go, and the reward.
  • A calmer city: fewer people on the streets, walking between real places and stopping a while when they arrive.
  • The landing pad is kept clear, nobody appears right next to you when you arrive, and passing remarks are rarer.

11 November 2026Version 2026.1111.0055

A living city

New
  • Port Halden has a crowd: people, service robots, sweepers, haulers, dock robots and drones, groups stopped to talk and people at the stalls.
  • Four street traders at stalls: noodles, weapon mods, surplus guns and drinks.
Better
  • People far out of view stop animating, which saves frame time.
Fixed
  • Sparks, steam vents and flickering lamps no longer flash inside the fog like lightning in a cloud.

Port Halden, the city

New
  • Port Halden is the hub: one city with the Nightjar on a landing pad, a market and the freight lift that starts every run.
  • Lifts from the depths bring you back up to the city, and quests point at the city and contract floors.

Fixed

31 October 2026Version 2026.1031.1355

Contracts, levels and a companion

New
  • Contracts: three floors with the Foundry Overseer at the bottom, across five tiers. Clearing one opens the next.
  • A free supply cache on contract floors: pick one of three mods or an overclock.
  • Character levels and XP from kills and finished runs, with a level and XP bar top left.
  • A companion follows you, fights beside you, draws fire and gets back up 10 seconds after going down. Rename it in the pause menu, or switch it off in Gameplay settings.
Better
  • HUD: health and the repair injector bottom left, Current and your abilities bottom right, the middle of the screen clear.
Balance
  • Dying on a run keeps your loot and half its XP, and you wake in Port Halden.
Fixed
  • The death screen names the floor you were on, and the minimap shows the right depth after loading a save.

Less loot, more range, playtest fixes

New

Better

30 October 2026Versions 2026.1030.1138, 2026.1030.1058

The loot update, the launcher and a new look

New
  • The Singularity launcher installs the game, keeps it up to date and starts it.
  • Legendary weapons roll a unique power: Detonator, Leech Relay, Current Siphon, Elite Hunter, Static Charge or Ricochet.
  • Mods drop from machines at every rarity. Fit them from the bag or by dragging onto a weapon or armour piece.
  • Weapon affixes that reward a style: Tracker suite, Point-blank rig, Fracture rounds, Salvage loop and Harvest coil.
  • Containers on dungeon floors: lockers, weapon cases, medical cabinets, supply crates and fallen workers.
  • Special weapons: the Ricochet Disc Launcher and the Gravity Well Projector, both in the ship armory.
  • Character appearance: body, skin, hood, nine hairstyles, six hair colours, facial hair and outfit dyes.
Better
  • 17 new gun models. Fitted mods show on the gun, and Rare or better guns carry a sight.
  • Dungeon floors carry lab consoles, specimen capsules, cargo and breakable crates.
  • Frame rate on deep floors is much higher (about 30 to about 49 fps at depth 18 on a fast Mac). Default render scale is 0.6.
  • A new HUD and inventory in gunmetal and emerald, with bigger ability slots and large cooldown numbers.
  • Item and character pictures lose their black boxes.
  • Readouts hold up on bright ground, and your character stands out from it.
  • New colours across the game: tech glows go emerald, Radiant goes gold and Prism green.
  • Launcher downloads can be paused, resume after a restart and retry by themselves after a dropped connection.
Balance
  • Machines never start a fight within 12 metres of the arrival lift.
  • Reinforcement waves stop growing past depth 6, though machines still get tougher.
  • The Relay Warden's enraged barrage is lighter.
Fixed
  • Dodging no longer turns you into a white ghost.
  • On a controller, the d-pad in the inventory no longer swaps weapons.
